About Me

Hello! I'm Simran, a passionate professional artist from Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh. For over 10+ years, I've been expressing emotions through colors on canvas.

My primary mediums are Acrylic, Oil Painting, and Watercolor. I focus mainly on nature, human emotions, Indian culture, and abstract art.

My goal is to touch the viewer's heart with every painting and inspire them to think deeply.

Exhibitions: Bhopal Art Fair 2024, Indore Creative Expo 2025

Awards: State Level Painting Competition - 1st Prize (2023)
